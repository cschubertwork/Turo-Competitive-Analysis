# Competitors

Tracked against a New Zealand market-entry framing lens for Turo, which has no current NZ presence. The set mixes NZ-specific car-share players (the direct terrain a launch would land on), an NZ-owned rental incumbent (the trust/localness bar to clear), and Turo's closest global peer-to-peer rival (a live lesson in what breaks this model's economics).

Note on ownership: Mevo and Zilch are both now owned by Carbn Group, following Mevo's 2026 collapse and acquisition. Two of the three NZ-specific profiles below sit under one parent; Cityhop (Toyota NZ) is the only NZ-specific player with a different, larger corporate backer.

| Name | Category | Website | Primary Segments | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mevo | Direct (NZ car-share) | https://mevo.co.nz/ | Wellington and Queenstown residents, occasional/car-free drivers | Free-floating model, minute-to-day rentals; collapsed into voluntary administration in March 2026 and was relaunched in Wellington by Carbn Group with a smaller Suzuki Swift fleet |
| Cityhop | Direct (NZ car-share) | https://cityhop.co.nz/ | Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch members | Back-to-base model, Toyota NZ-owned since 2018 |
| Zilch (formerly Yoogo Share) | Direct (NZ car-share) | https://www.zilch.nz/ | Business and personal EV subscribers in five cities | Back-to-base EV fleet, longer-term subscription model, rebranded from Yoogo Share; owned by Carbn Group, the same parent that acquired Mevo out of administration in 2026 |
| GO Rentals | Adjacent (NZ traditional rental) | https://www.gorentals.co.nz/ | Domestic and international travelers | NZ-owned rental incumbent, chosen over an international agency brand as the more informative "local trust and reputation" comparison |
| Getaround | Aspirational-turned-cautionary (global P2P) | https://www.getaround.com/ | Global P2P car-share guests and hosts | Turo's closest global peer-to-peer rival by model; board voted to dissolve and liquidate in June 2026 after already exiting the US in 2025. Kept in the set as a concrete case study in what breaks this model's unit economics at scale, not as an active threat |

## Deliberately left out

- International rental agency brands operating in NZ (Avis, Hertz, Budget, Europcar, Sixt) - as a group, they compete on the same axis as GO Rentals (fleet-owned, agency-model rental) but add little beyond it individually. GO Rentals was chosen as the single incumbent profile because the "local, NZ-owned, better-regarded" positioning is the sharper comparison for a new entrant than a generic international brand.
