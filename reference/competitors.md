# Competitors

Tracked against a New Zealand market-entry framing lens for Turo, which has no current NZ presence. The set mixes NZ-specific car-share players (the direct terrain a launch would land on), New Zealand's only live peer-to-peer vehicle marketplace (Camplify, for campervans), an NZ-owned rental incumbent, and Turo's closest global peer-to-peer rival (a marketplace like Turo that failed anyway).

Note on ownership (Companies Office register, checked 2026-10-03): Mevo and Zilch are both owned by Carbn Group, which bought Mevo's brand and assets out of administration in 2026; trade press reported in June 2026 that Carbn is becoming a subsidiary of Custom Fleet, which the register does not yet show. Cityhop trades as KINTO New Zealand Limited, owned by Toyota Finance New Zealand (Toyota Financial Services), which also owns Ezi Car Rental.

## Deliberately left out

- International rental agency brands operating in NZ (Avis, Hertz, Budget, Europcar, Sixt) - as a group, they compete on the same axis as GO Rentals (fleet-owned, agency-model rental) but add little beyond it individually. GO Rentals was chosen as the single incumbent profile because the "local, NZ-owned, better-regarded" positioning is the sharper comparison for a new entrant than a generic international brand.
- Ezi Car Rental, now owned by Toyota Financial Services alongside Cityhop and top of Canstar Blue's 2026 hire-car satisfaction ranking. It is fleet-model rental, the same category GO Rentals already represents, but it is the first profile to add if the set grows.
- New Zealand's earlier peer-to-peer car platforms, YourDrive (2012 to 2020, half-owned by Jucy) and MyCarYourRental (its site no longer loads as of 2026-10-03). Neither is operating, so they appear in the analysis as precedents rather than as profiles.
