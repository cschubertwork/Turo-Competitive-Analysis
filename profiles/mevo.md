# Mevo

## Summary

*Category: Direct (NZ car-share) | Depth: full | Researched: 2026-09-22*

| Dimension | Mevo | Turo |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Free-floating, minute-to-day car share | Peer-to-peer marketplace, host-owned cars |
| Geography | Wellington, Queenstown (post-collapse; was 4 NZ cities) | US, Canada, UK, France, Australia |
| Status | Relaunched after March 2026 collapse | Operating, EBITDA-positive |

### 1) Snapshot

- Inferred (assumption): secondary sources describe Mevo as founded in Wellington in 2014 by Erik Zydervelt and Finn Lawrence; this could not be verbatim-confirmed against a fetched primary source in this research, so it is held as unconfirmed rather than Observed.
- Observed: at its peak, Mevo operated across four cities with a mixed fleet including Teslas, Polestars, and Suzuki Swifts. (https://autotalk.co.nz/mevo-enters-voluntary-administration-weeks-after-3-28m-crowdfunding-raise/)
- Observed: Mevo expanded into Hamilton in 2021 by acquiring Loop from Ebbett Group. (https://autotalk.co.nz/mevo-enters-voluntary-administration-weeks-after-3-28m-crowdfunding-raise/)
- Observed: Mevo Limited and Mevo Assets were placed into voluntary administration on 30 March 2026, weeks after raising NZ$3.28 million through equity crowdfunding platform Snowball Effect, a raise that had exceeded its NZ$2.2 million target. (https://autotalk.co.nz/mevo-enters-voluntary-administration-weeks-after-3-28m-crowdfunding-raise/)
- Observed: an investor was set to give Mevo NZ$1.7 million that would have seen it through administration, but pulled out because Mevo breached some conditions of the deal. (https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/business/591275/mevo-car-sharing-service-goes-into-voluntary-administration)
- Inferred (assumption): coverage reports that Nelson lost its car-sharing service after Mevo's exit, with a councillor quoted on the fragility of the model; this could not be verbatim-confirmed against a fetched source in this research (the relevant pages returned access errors), so it is held as unconfirmed.
- Observed: Carbn was founded in July 2020. (https://nzbusiness.co.nz/technology/acquisition-targets-low-carbon-fleet-transition)
- Observed: Carbn Group Holdings has acquired the brand and assets of collapsed car-sharing operator Mevo. (https://autotalk.co.nz/carbn-group-acquires-mevo-to-relaunch-wellington-car-sharing/)
- Observed: the Mevo acquisition aligns with Carbn Group's existing Zilch network of 20 shared-vehicle electric hubs across New Zealand's major centres, meaning Carbn already owned Zilch before acquiring Mevo. (https://autotalk.co.nz/carbn-group-acquires-mevo-to-relaunch-wellington-car-sharing/)
- Observed: the Wellington relaunch features a fleet of Suzuki Swifts operating under the Mevo brand, with plans for national expansion and a broader range of electric vehicle types as the business develops. (https://autotalk.co.nz/carbn-group-acquires-mevo-to-relaunch-wellington-car-sharing/)
- Observed: Mevo currently operates in Wellington and Queenstown only. (https://mevo.co.nz/)
- Inferred (assumption): Mevo's pre-collapse fleet mix (Teslas, Polestars) was likely too capital-intensive to run profitably at its scale; the relaunch's shift to cheaper Suzuki Swifts reads as a direct correction to that.
- Inferred (assumption): raising more than its crowdfunding target and still collapsing within weeks suggests the failure was a short-term cash-flow and timing problem (the specific fallen-through NZ$1.7M commitment) rather than an inability to attract capital in general.

### 2) NZ Regulatory & Insurance Environment

- Inferred (assumption): pre-collapse Mevo support content described a standard insurance excess of NZ$4,000 per trip, selectable as Standard, Enhanced, or Total Cover, with a weekly add-on reducing the excess to $0; this could not be verbatim-confirmed against a fetched page in this research (the support site returned a certificate error), so it is held as unconfirmed. It is also unclear whether these terms still apply post-relaunch.
- Inferred (assumption): reporting on Mevo's Auckland launch approval described a Wellington City Council parking arrangement, but this research could not verbatim-confirm the specific "Home Zone" cost-neutral parking terms against a fetched source.

### 3) Brand-Building & Local Marketing Channels

- Observed: Mevo's Automobile Association (AA) partnership offers AA members up to NZ$100 in free Mevo credit in their first year, covering Auckland, Wellington, and Hamilton. (https://autotalk.co.nz/car-share-operator-mevo-joins-forces-with-the-automobile-association/)
- Observed: Mevo's fleet was owned and maintained by Mevo and powered by Meridian Energy. (https://www.meridianenergy.co.nz/ev/mevo)
- Inferred (assumption): Z Energy is reported to have held a stake of up to 32% in the pre-collapse Mevo; this could not be verbatim-confirmed against a fetched source in this research.
- Inferred (assumption): no sports sponsorship or event partnership turned up anywhere in research on Mevo, across both its pre-collapse and relaunched incarnations. Its brand-building has run through motoring-body and energy-company partnerships (AA, Meridian, and reportedly Z Energy) rather than sport or culture.

### 4) Local Competitive Tension

- Observed: pre-collapse, Mevo was described in press coverage as "Wellington car-sharing king" when Auckland Transport policy changes opened the door to its Auckland launch. (https://www.nzherald.co.nz/business/companies/freight-logistics/new-at-policy-sees-wellington-car-sharing-king-mevo-hit-go-for-auckland-launch/KN5VOZHI5AJQBZVKQUEKWMOIZ4/)
- Inferred (assumption): Mevo's collapse despite being called the Wellington market leader, and despite an over-subscribed funding round, is the single most important data point in this whole analysis for reading Turo's own margin discipline against a New Zealand launch. Local market leadership did not translate into a business that could survive one funding delay.

### 5) Fleet, Coverage & EV Mix

- Observed: the Wellington relaunch fleet operates under the Mevo brand as Suzuki Swifts. (https://autotalk.co.nz/carbn-group-acquires-mevo-to-relaunch-wellington-car-sharing/)
- Observed: pre-collapse, Mevo ran fifteen plug-in hybrid Audi A3 e-trons around Wellington CBD and Wellington International Airport. (https://thespinoff.co.nz/business/14-03-2018/the-primer-the-car-sharing-service-putting-electric-vehicles-on-wellingtons-roads)
- Observed: New Zealand Herald coverage of Mevo's Auckland expansion describes the Auckland Transport policy change as authorising a "free-floating" car-share model for Mevo, meaning vehicles can be picked up and left within a zone rather than returned to a fixed base. (https://www.nzherald.co.nz/business/companies/freight-logistics/new-at-policy-sees-wellington-car-sharing-king-mevo-hit-go-for-auckland-launch/KN5VOZHI5AJQBZVKQUEKWMOIZ4/)
- Inferred (assumption): exact current fleet size (vehicle count) for Wellington or Queenstown was not published in any source found in this research; Carbn Group has not disclosed a number publicly.

### 6) Pricing & Packaging

- Observed: current Wellington pricing runs three tiers: Compact at NZ$19/hour or NZ$95/day, Comfort at NZ$22/hour or NZ$110/day, and Premium at NZ$25/hour or NZ$125/day. (https://mevo.co.nz/wellington/pricing)
- Observed: the first 100km of each trip is free, with additional distance charged at NZ$0.39/km. (https://mevo.co.nz/wellington/pricing)
- Observed: charging, parking, and insurance are always included in the price. (https://mevo.co.nz/wellington/pricing)
- Observed: there is no fixed membership or joining fee mentioned in Mevo's pricing pages; the model is pay-as-you-go by the hour or day. (https://mevo.co.nz/wellington/pricing)

### 7) Differentiators vs Turo

- Inferred (assumption): Mevo is stronger than Turo on immediacy and short-trip convenience: free-floating pickup anywhere in its zone beats Turo's model of booking a specific host's car in advance, for a same-day or spontaneous single trip.
- Inferred (assumption): Turo is stronger than Mevo on selection and trip length: Turo's marketplace of individually owned cars spans far more vehicle types and easily supports multi-day trips, while Mevo's relaunched fleet is a single, deliberately cheap vehicle type (Suzuki Swift).
- Inferred (assumption): Turo is structurally stronger on capital risk: it owns no fleet at all, so it carries none of the vehicle-financing exposure that appears to have driven Mevo's collapse. Any NZ entry Turo made would inherit this same asset-light advantage over Mevo's model.
- Inferred (assumption): Mevo and Turo are roughly on par on trust-building tools (ID verification, in-app insurance selection), based on the insurance mechanics found for each.

### 8) Risks for Turo

- Inferred (assumption): Mevo's brand damage from a public collapse could make New Zealand consumers more skeptical of any new vehicle-access startup, including a Turo entry, at least in the near term after March 2026.
- Inferred (assumption): Mevo's existing council relationships (the Wellington City Council "Home Zone" parking deal) and AA partnership are an incumbent advantage that a new entrant, including Turo, would have to negotiate from scratch.
- Inferred (assumption): Z Energy's and Meridian Energy's stakes in Mevo give it access to fuel and charging infrastructure deals that Turo, as an out-of-market entrant, would not have on day one.

### 9) Opportunities for Turo

- Inferred (assumption): Mevo's retreat from Auckland, Hamilton, and Nelson leaves those specific cities without the free-floating car-share option they briefly had, which is a gap Turo's marketplace model (no fleet to place city by city) could fill without the capital risk that sank Mevo.
- Inferred (assumption): Mevo's collapse is a concrete, recent, local cautionary tale that Turo could use in its own market-entry narrative or investor conversations to differentiate its asset-light model from fleet-owning NZ operators.
- Inferred (assumption): no NZ car-share operator found in this research, Mevo included, uses sports sponsorship for brand-building. That is open ground for a new entrant, not a channel Turo would need to fight an incumbent for.

### 10) Open Questions

- Whether the relaunched Mevo has settled on a fleet size or a firm city-expansion timeline beyond the Wellington Suzuki Swift relaunch and the existing Queenstown operation.
- Whether Carbn Group intends to keep Mevo and Zilch as separate consumer brands or eventually merge them.
- What Mevo's live insurance and parking terms are post-relaunch, since the pre-collapse terms found in support articles may not carry over unchanged.
- The exact vehicle count and membership numbers for both Wellington and Queenstown today; no source in this research disclosed a current figure.
