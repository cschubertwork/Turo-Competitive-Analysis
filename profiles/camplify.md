# Camplify

## Summary

*Category: Direct (NZ peer-to-peer campervan marketplace) | Depth: survey | Researched: 2026-10-03*

| Dimension | Camplify | Turo |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Peer-to-peer campervan/motorhome/caravan marketplace, asset-light (owners list their own RVs, no fleet) | Peer-to-peer car marketplace, asset-light (hosts list their own cars) |
| Geography | New Zealand (nationwide), Australia, UK, Spain, Germany, Netherlands, Austria | US, Canada, UK, France, Australia |
| Backing | ASX-listed Camplify Holdings Limited (ASX: CHL) | People Inc., formerly IAC (~33%) |

### 1) Snapshot

- Observed: Camplify's New Zealand site positions itself as "New Zealand's favourite RV hire marketplace," advertising "2,000+ RVs from local Kiwi owners" across three vehicle categories only: campervan, motorhome and caravan hire. No car, SUV or sedan category appears anywhere in its homepage navigation, vehicle-type copy or FAQ text. (https://www.camplify.co.nz/)
- Observed: "Having grown an audience of over 60,000 members across the UK and Australia, Camplify expanded to New Zealand in 2019, to help make Vanlife accessible around the world." (https://www.camplify.co.nz/about)
- Observed: in March 2022 the NZ Commerce Commission cleared Camplify Co (NZ) Limited, a subsidiary of ASX-listed Camplify Holdings Limited, "to acquire the New Zealand assets and business of Tourism Holdings Limited's (THL's) peer-to-peer motorhome and campervan rental platforms, Mighway and SHAREaCAMPER." The deal completed on 2 May 2022: Camplify's own ASX announcement states the acquisition "has now been completed," that it "positions Camplify as the largest operator in the New Zealand market," and that it "will bring an additional 800 plus vehicles to the Camplify platform in New Zealand." (https://www.comcom.govt.nz/news-and-media/news-and-events/2022/commission-clears-camplify-to-acquire-thls-mighway-and-shareacamper/ ; https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20220502/pdf/458l43wzdzgf77.pdf)
- Observed: Camplify's NZ owner page states it is "free to join and list your van" with "No upfront costs: only pay when you receive a booking," "From 7% commission," and that "thousands of Kiw RV owners are earning up to $10,000" a year sharing their vehicle on Camplify. (https://www.camplify.co.nz/for-owners)
- Observed: Camplify's parent is described in its own FY25 ASX results announcement as "Camplify Holdings Limited (ASX:CHL) is in the business of elevating outdoor experiences through innovative and scalable tech solutions. Composed of Camplify, MyWay and PaulCamper, the CHL Group operates one of the world's leading peer-to-peer digital marketplace platforms." The same announcement reports "Group Revenue: $42.1 million (FY24: $47.8 million)" and "Net Loss After Tax: $15.8 million (FY24: $8.1 million), inclusive of a $6.0m non-cash impairment of goodwill." (https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20250828/pdf/06nhk816c69ydk.pdf)

### 2) NZ Regulatory & Insurance Environment

- Observed: owners get "Damage protection and legal liability (including public liability) through MyWay Mutual, a member-owned organisation," described elsewhere on the same page as "Myway Protect... damage protection designed specifically for RV owners." (https://www.camplify.co.nz/for-owners)
- Observed: Camplify's FY25 results announcement confirms this insurer is new: a "Key Achievement in FY25" was that it "Completed the migration of customers on membership protection from previous insurer to Myway Mutual," and that "the new mutual program replaced third-party products in Australia and New Zealand (ANZ)" in its first two months of operation. (https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20250828/pdf/06nhk816c69ydk.pdf)
- Observed: "24/7 breakdown cover: Our trusted roadside partners provide 24/7 nationwide breakdown cover for all Camplify bookings." The partner is not named on the page. (https://www.camplify.co.nz/insurance)
- Observed: "Hirers must be over 21 and under 80 years of age, and have a full drivers license," with some vans carrying "a higher minimum age of 25." Separately: "Do I need a special licence to drive a campervan? No. A standard New Zealand Class 1 (car) licence is all you need," but for motorhomes, "Most motorhomes can be driven on a standard Class 1 licence (up to 6,000kg GVM, or up to 4,500kg GVM if towing). Larger motorhomes above that threshold require a Class 2 licence." (https://www.camplify.co.nz/insurance ; https://www.camplify.co.nz/)
- Observed: as eligibility criteria for its 2026/27 summer "Income Guarantee" listing promotion, Camplify states a qualifying RV must have a "Valid Certificate of Fitness" for a "Rental (drivable vehicles) or Warrant of Fitness (towable vehicles)," alongside being "Certified Self-Contained (valid green warrant)." This is stated only as promo eligibility wording, not as a general, standalone statement of NZ owner-licensing law. (https://www.camplify.co.nz/for-owners)

### 3) Brand-Building & Local Marketing Channels

- Observed: Camplify's NZ homepage leads with "Rated 4.9/5 with 13,142 reviews" and tells hirers "Hirers have their licence and identity verified before any booking is confirmed," while its owner page adds "All members are verified. All hirers go through licence and ID checks to give you peace of mind." (https://www.camplify.co.nz/ ; https://www.camplify.co.nz/for-owners)
- Observed: Camplify's brand-partnerships page breaks down NZ trip-takers by nationality: "The Kiwi locals 38.7% of trips. Six nights, $1,820... The Aussies 28.8% of all NZ trips, nearly half of everything inbound. Ten nights, $2,715, and a three-hour flight," alongside "61% fly in for this Six in ten NZ trips are booked by an international traveller." (https://www.camplify.co.nz/brand-partnerships)
- Inferred (assumption): no sports, team or event sponsorship turned up in searches of the NZ site; the brand-partnerships page is itself a sponsorship pitch rather than a record of an existing sponsor, opening with "Come on a road trip with New Zealand's largest RV community Over 40,000 RV owners, 500,000 holidaymakers - and a spare seat for your brand at the front. Get in touch." No named sports team, athlete or event sponsorship was found on this page or elsewhere on the NZ site. (https://www.camplify.co.nz/brand-partnerships)

### 4) Local Competitive Tension

- Observed: Camplify's FY25 results announcement's "Market performance" table reports, for New Zealand versus its other markets (Australia, UK, Spain, Germany, Netherlands, Austria): "GTV Growth... +10%... Revenue Growth... +10%... Booking Growth... +9%... Fleet Growth... +26%," the only market in the table with growth across all four metrics (Australia's GTV, revenue and bookings all declined that year). (https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20250828/pdf/06nhk816c69ydk.pdf)
- Observed: the same report notes "The majority of GTV was generated in Australia (49.9%) with Germany remaining the 2nd largest market (25.3%). NZ growth has increased its share of total GTV to 15.6% while other markets remained relatively unchanged," implying New Zealand is now Camplify's third-largest market by that measure. (https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20250828/pdf/06nhk816c69ydk.pdf)
- Inferred (assumption): with Mighway and SHAREaCAMPER folded into Camplify since May 2022, there is no remaining independent peer-to-peer campervan/motorhome platform of scale in New Zealand; a Turo host or partner looking to compete in this specific vehicle class would be negotiating or competing against Camplify directly, not choosing among several NZ-specific P2P RV marketplaces.
