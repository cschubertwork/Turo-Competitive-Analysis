### 1) Snapshot

- Observed: Getaround operated a peer-to-peer car-sharing marketplace with the same basic model as Turo: private owners list vehicles, guests book them, Getaround takes a commission. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000095017024038301/getr-20231231.htm)
- Observed: Getaround operated in France, Germany, Spain, Austria, Belgium, the UK, and Norway, in addition to the United States, 8 countries in total. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000095017024038301/getr-20231231.htm)
- Observed: Getaround ran the same zero-fleet marketplace model as Turo, never owning or financing vehicles. It recorded net losses of $136.1 million (2022) and $113.9 million (2023), against total revenues of $72.7 million in 2023, so losses exceeded revenue, and its 10-K disclosed "substantial doubt about the ability of the Company to continue in existence as a going concern," funded in part by the Mudrick Super Priority Note, a senior secured obligation held by a single creditor and secured against substantially all of the company's assets. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000095017024038301/getr-20231231.htm)
- Observed: effective 16 August 2024, Getaround's common stock was delisted from the NYSE for falling below the $15 million minimum average market capitalisation standard, a delisting that constituted a "Fundamental Change" under its Convertible Notes and required a forbearance agreement with Mudrick to avoid default. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000114036126026155/ny20075435x2_def14a.htm)
- Observed: on 7 February 2025, Getaround's board approved an orderly, phased wind-down of its US car-share and HyreCar operations, citing insufficient cash flow and an inability to secure additional funding, with the wind-down expected to be substantially complete by the end of 2025; its European business continued operating. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000095017025018225/getr-20250207.htm and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000114036126026155/ny20075435x2_def14a.htm)
- Observed: in April 2026, Getaround sold its European car-sharing business to Denmark-based GoMore ApS for approximately 31.5 million euros in cash plus a promissory note. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000119312526263769/d62341d8k.htm)
- Observed: on 5 June 2026, Getaround's board determined that, with no operating business left and no revenue source, dissolution and liquidation were in the company's best interests. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000119312526263769/d62341d8k.htm)
- Observed: Getaround's board and stockholders approved a formal Delaware dissolution and liquidation plan in June and July 2026, and the company does not expect any liquidating distribution to stockholders. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000119312526332893/d155130d8k.htm)
- Observed: effective 26 August 2026, Getaround's entire board of directors resigned and appointed Craig Jalbert, a restructuring specialist, as the company's sole director and sole officer to carry out the dissolution. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000119312526378368/d508106d8k.htm)
- Observed: as of 30 September 2024, Getaround's trailing twelve-month revenue was $76.9 million, roughly 8% of Turo's $936.1 million trailing-twelve-month net revenue over the same period. (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839608/000095017024126782/getr-20240930.htm and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1514587/000162828024047889/turoinc-sx1a12.htm)
