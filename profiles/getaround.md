### 1) Snapshot

- Observed: Getaround operated a peer-to-peer car-sharing marketplace with the same basic model as Turo: private owners list vehicles, guests book them, Getaround takes a commission. (https://businessmodelanalyst.com/getaround-business-model/)
- Observed: Getaround operated in France, Germany, Spain, Austria, Belgium, the UK, Norway, and the Netherlands, in addition to the United States. (https://tracxn.com/d/companies/getaround/__rKTuwF38V_98nVu1zJDgX-nIsauBE9z_Ekb5vtj1trE)
- Observed: in February 2025, Getaround ceased its US car-sharing operations, citing an ongoing lack of liquidity, while keeping its European business running. (https://carcody.com/getaround-abruptly-shut-down-its-u-s-car-sharing-operations-what-happened/)
- Observed: in April 2026, Getaround sold its European car-sharing business to Denmark-based GoMore ApS for approximately 31.5 million euros in cash plus a promissory note. (https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/GETR/8-k-getaround-inc-reports-material-event-83400e038c70.html)
- Observed: on 5 June 2026, Getaround's board determined that, with no operating business left and no revenue source, dissolution and liquidation were in the company's best interests. (https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/getaround-plans-formal-dissolution-and-liquidation-process)
- Observed: Getaround's board and stockholders approved a formal Delaware dissolution and liquidation plan in June and July 2026, and the company does not expect any liquidating distribution to stockholders. (https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/getaround-stockholders-approve-company-dissolution-and-liquidation)
- Observed: as of 30 September 2024, Getaround's trailing twelve-month revenue was $76.9 million, roughly 8% of Turo's scale over a comparable period. (https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/52765-30)
