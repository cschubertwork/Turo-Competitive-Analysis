### 1) Snapshot

- Observed: GO Rentals is one of New Zealand's largest independent car rental companies, with ten branches across Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Nelson, Queenstown, Dunedin, and Invercargill. (https://pacificdestinations.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/GO-Rentals-Trade-Guide-2024-amended1.pdf)
- Observed: from 1 August 2022, GO Rentals holds the licence to operate the Enterprise Rent-A-Car, National Car Rental, and Alamo Rent A Car brands in New Zealand, extending global network ties. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/explore/news/go-rentals-partners-with-worlds-largest-rental-car-company/)
- Observed: the fleet includes electric and hybrid vehicles alongside conventional cars, ranging from the Tesla Model Y and Toyota C-HR to the Ford Everest Titanium and Mitsubishi Pajero Sport. (https://pacificdestinations.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/GO-Rentals-Trade-Guide-2024-amended1.pdf)
- Observed: GO Rentals has won New Zealand Tourism Awards for Visitor Experience and Canstar Blue's Most Satisfied Customers Award in 2018, 2020, 2022, and 2023. (https://pacificdestinations.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/GO-Rentals-Trade-Guide-2024-amended1.pdf)
- Observed: GO Rentals is a Toitu-certified business and integrates the Tiaki sustainable-tourism promise across the business. (https://pacificdestinations.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/GO-Rentals-Trade-Guide-2024-amended1.pdf)

### 2) NZ Regulatory & Insurance Environment

- Observed: GO Rentals includes comprehensive cover as standard, with third-party property damage cover up to NZ$10,000,000 and third-party injury liability cover up to NZ$10,000,000. (https://carrentalhero.co.nz/en/guides/rental-car-insurance-excess-nz)
- Observed: windscreen and tyre damage are covered under GO Rentals' standard vehicle and third-party damage protection, unlike some rental agencies that exclude them. (https://carrentalhero.co.nz/en/guides/rental-car-insurance-excess-nz)
- Inferred (assumption): GO Rentals does not publish its excess amount on its own website; a third-party comparison notes this explicitly, so the exact figure would need to come from a live quote or the rental agreement.

### 3) Brand-Building & Local Marketing Channels

- Observed: GO Rentals' brand-building found in this pass runs through tourism-industry recognition (Tourism Awards, Canstar Blue) and its Enterprise Holdings global-brand licence rather than sponsorship. (https://pacificdestinations.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/GO-Rentals-Trade-Guide-2024-amended1.pdf)
- Inferred (assumption): no sponsorship of a New Zealand sports team, athlete, or event turned up in this pass; GO Rentals' visible brand-building leans on service-quality awards and tourism-trade credibility rather than cultural or sporting association.

### 4) Local Competitive Tension

- Observed: GO Rentals is positioned in market coverage as a local operator alongside Snap Rentals and Ezi Car Rental, contrasted with international brands like Hertz (43 NZ locations) and Avis. (https://www.dreambigtravelfarblog.com/blog/car-rentals-new-zealand)
- Inferred (assumption): local NZ-owned operators including GO Rentals are reported to score higher on customer service than international brands, which is the specific trust and localness bar a new P2P entrant would also need to clear, not just a price comparison against international agencies.
