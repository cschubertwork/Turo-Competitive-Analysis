### 1) Snapshot

- Observed: GO Rentals is New Zealand's largest independent car rental company, with 10 branches: Auckland City, Auckland Airport, Waiheke Island, Wellington Airport, Nelson Airport, Greymouth Station, Christchurch Airport, Queenstown Airport, Dunedin Airport, and Invercargill Airport. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/rental-car-locations/, https://www.gorentals.co.nz/about-us/endorsements/)
- Observed: from 1 August 2022, GO Rentals holds the licence to operate the Enterprise Rent-A-Car, National Car Rental, and Alamo Rent A Car brands in New Zealand, extending global network ties. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/explore/news/go-rentals-partners-with-worlds-largest-rental-car-company/)
- Observed: GO Rentals' fleet mixes electric and hybrid vehicles with conventional cars, and currently includes the Tesla Model Y and the Mitsubishi Pajero Sport. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/tesla-rental-nz/, https://www.gorentals.co.nz/vehicles/4-wheel-drives/)
- Observed: GO Rentals has won Canstar Blue's Most Satisfied Customers Award in 2018, 2020, 2022, and 2023, and the NZ Tourism Awards Visitor Experience Award in 2018. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/about-us/endorsements/)
- Observed: GO Rentals is Toitū net Carbonzero certified and integrates the Tiaki sustainable-tourism promise across the business. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/about-us/endorsements/)

### 2) NZ Regulatory & Insurance Environment

- Observed: GO Rentals includes comprehensive cover as standard, with third-party property damage cover up to NZ$10,000,000 and third-party injury liability cover up to NZ$10,000,000. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/services/rental-car-insurance/)
- Observed: windscreen and tyre damage are covered under GO Rentals' standard vehicle and third-party damage protection, included identically across all three of its insurance tiers. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/services/rental-car-insurance/)
- Observed: GO Rentals discloses a three-tier insurance structure (GO Basic, GO Assured, GO Peace of Mind), but does not publish a dollar excess figure for GO Basic or GO Assured; excess on those tiers is described as vehicle-dependent, and GO Peace of Mind reduces it to zero. (https://www.gorentals.co.nz/services/rental-car-insurance/)

### 3) Brand-Building & Local Marketing Channels

- Inferred (assumption): GO Rentals' brand-building found in this pass runs through tourism-industry recognition (Tourism Awards, Canstar Blue) and its Enterprise Holdings global-brand licence rather than sponsorship.
- Inferred (assumption): no sponsorship of a New Zealand sports team, athlete, or event turned up in this pass; GO Rentals' visible brand-building leans on service-quality awards and tourism-trade credibility rather than cultural or sporting association.

### 4) Local Competitive Tension

- Observed: Canstar's 2026 car-hire customer satisfaction survey (fieldwork finalised June 2026, published July 2026) ranks GO Rentals fourth of seven brands, behind local operator Ezi Car Rental and international brands Budget and Hertz, and ahead of local operator Apex and international brands Avis and Thrifty. (https://www.canstar.co.nz/car-insurance/hire-cars/)
- Observed: GO Rentals won Canstar Blue's Most Satisfied Customers award four times, in 2018, 2020, 2022, and 2023, more than any other brand, but has not won since: Budget won in 2024 and Snap Rentals in 2025. (https://www.canstar.co.nz/car-insurance/hire-cars/)
- Inferred (assumption): GO Rentals' NZ ownership has not kept it ahead of international brands on customer satisfaction; Canstar's current ranking puts it behind Budget and Hertz, despite GO winning the award more often than any other brand from 2018 to 2023. The trust bar a new P2P entrant needs to clear looks more like consistent service delivery than local ownership alone.
